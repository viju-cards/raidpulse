Up and running in 3 steps

On stream in just a few minutes.

1
Download & unzip

Unzip the archive, run Overlay.exe.

2
Addon into WoW

Put the BossPixelExport folder into Interface/AddOns, then /reload. Important: the pixels at the bottom-left of the screen must not be covered by chat.

3
Add to OBS

Browser source: http://127.0.0.1:8787/overlay.html (1920×360, at the bottom).

+
Optional: import pull counts

Import your guild's current pull count once via WarcraftLogs (in settings under "WarcraftLogs Import").

The full guide is also right inside the app under "Setup / Guide".
